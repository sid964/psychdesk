Not every psychological intervention has an immediate, reversible effect.","Skills learned during treatment may continue after withdrawal, so the absence of a return to baseline does not automatically show that the intervention was ineffective.","A potentially dangerous symptom should not be deliberately worsened for the sake of a cleaner experimental pattern.","Different research methods address different questions. Case studies reveal rich individual patterns, correlational designs test associations, experiments manipulate variables to examine causation, and single-case designs track changes over time. Genetic and longitudinal strategies examine other dimensions of vulnerability and development. When both interventions produce improvement, the crucial question may concern relative benefit, adverse effects, cost, acceptability or which patients respond best. Conclusions require adequate sample size, fair delivery and meaningful outcome measures.
