Assessment ordinarily integrates an interview with observations and, when justified, psychological, medical or biological measures. Decisions should follow the clinical question. Validity concerns whether the interpretation and intended use of results are supported by evidence. Standardization involves administering, scoring and interpreting measures through consistent procedures, often with appropriate reference information.
