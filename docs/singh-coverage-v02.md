# PsychDesk · A. K. Singh — coverage report

**Package v0.2.0 — all contents-page teaching topics drafted; full academic audit pending.**

This supersedes v0.1.0, but is not certified as a complete replacement for the textbook. The 63 original lessons are preserved (their teaching prose, paragraph anchors and identifiers are unchanged). 159 additional main-topic lessons were authored for chapters 9–24, all with revision and recall. The source transcript was reopened and relevant chapter previews, passages and research/statistical sections examined. The PDF was directly reopened and printed-page anchors and selected educational figures checked visually. The full 514-page PDF has not been read and verified paragraph by paragraph. Some general methodological clarifications, worked examples and contemporary cautionary statements are companion-created teaching material, not direct textbook claims; their exact source correspondence remains pending. No external research sources were added.

## Source and scan limitations

- A. K. Singh, *Tests, Measurements and Research Methods in Behavioural Sciences*. Title and author from the supplied cover. Edition, publisher, year and ISBN are unverified (no reliable imprint page found). The OCR/Markdown and PDF are the supplied source pair; the actual PDF contains 514 viewer pages, in contrast to the indexed preview exposing 463.
- The PDF uses mixed single-page scans and two-page spreads, including rotated pages. The mapping is piecewise; 79 printed-page anchors were recorded as visually checked, including the prior checkpoint anchors. **Anchor checks verify pagination, not all source heading locations or teaching claims.** Inferred links remain explicitly marked.
- Scan discontinuities identified: after printed p. 679, the objective questions start on p. 683; after p. 708, appendix A begins p. 711; after p. 713, the glossary begins p. 753. The supplied file ends at p. 774. Thus printed pp. 680–682, 709–710, 714–752, 775–776 and the index beginning p. 777 are not present in the supplied scan (some may be blank or divider pages; content cannot be assumed). The available objective-question, appendix and glossary sections are indexed, not fully extracted.
- Historical versions of intelligence and personality instruments are treated as historical. Actual clinical use requires current authorized manuals and appropriate professional qualifications. Historical report formatting is not a substitute for current university style instructions.
