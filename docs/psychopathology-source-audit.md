## Limitations

The user-supplied Markdown contains numerous garbled and interleaved text columns. Page numbers and headings were compared with its table of contents and readable passages; doubtful OCR fragments were not used to author additional facts. The app contains **six introductory Chapter 1 lessons plus three sub-concepts**; chapters 2–16 are an accurately mapped outline, **not** completed course content. A later content expansion requires source-by-source review of those chapters.
